About

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Peking University, advised by Bin Dong.

My research interests include high-dimensional and multi-source data analysis; PDEs, semiparametric methods, and complex modeling; deep learning and large language models; and AI for statistics and machine learning.
